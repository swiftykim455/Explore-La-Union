Featured Destinations

Five amazing places you should discover in La Union.

01

📍 San Juan, La Union

San Juan Beach

San Juan Beach is one of La Union's popular coastal destinations. It is known for surfing, beautiful sunsets, relaxing beach views, and a lively tourism atmosphere.

Major Attractions

  • Surfing waves
  • Beach scenery
  • Sunset views
  • Nearby cafés and restaurants

Recommended Activities

🏄 Surfing🌅 Sunset Viewing📸 Photography🏖️ Beach Activities

Best time: Dry season and suitable weather conditions.

02

📍 San Fernando City

Ma-Cho Temple

Ma-Cho Temple is a Taoist temple located on a hill overlooking the sea. Its architecture, religious significance, and elevated location make it an interesting cultural destination.

Major Attractions

  • Taoist architecture
  • Sea views
  • Cultural heritage
  • Peaceful surroundings

Recommended Activities

🏯 Sightseeing🙏 Cultural Visit📸 Photography

Best time: Daytime, while respecting temple rules.

03

📍 San Gabriel, La Union

Tangadan Falls

Tangadan Falls is a beautiful natural waterfall surrounded by lush landscapes. Reaching the falls involves an outdoor trek, making it ideal for adventure seekers.

Major Attractions

  • Waterfall
  • Natural pools
  • Mountain scenery
  • Forest trails

Recommended Activities

🥾 Trekking💦 Swimming🌿 Nature Trip📸 Photography

Best time: When weather and trail conditions are safe.

04

📍 Luna, La Union

Bahay na Bato

Bahay na Bato is an artistic attraction in Luna featuring stone sculptures, creative artworks, and a beautiful coastal environment.

Major Attractions

  • Stone sculptures
  • Local artwork
  • Coastal scenery
  • Unique architecture

Recommended Activities

🎨 Art Appreciation📸 Photography🚶 Sightseeing

Best time: Morning or afternoon.

05

📍 San Fernando City

Poro Point Lighthouse

Poro Point Lighthouse is a historic coastal landmark in San Fernando. It provides visitors an opportunity to appreciate the area's maritime heritage and coastal landscape.

Major Attractions

  • Historic lighthouse
  • Coastal views
  • Maritime heritage
  • Photography spots

Recommended Activities

📸 Photography🏛️ History🌊 Sightseeing

Best time: Daytime and clear weather.

TRAVEL RESPONSIBLY

Travel Tips

Stay safe while protecting La Union's people, culture, and environment.

🛡️

Safety First

Check weather conditions before visiting outdoor destinations. Follow warning signs and instructions from local authorities.

♻️

Keep It Clean

Do not litter. Bring reusable items when possible and dispose of waste properly.

🤝

Respect Local Culture

Respect local traditions, religious places, communities, and local residents.

🌊

Protect Nature

Avoid damaging plants, rocks, wildlife, waterfalls, beaches, and other natural attractions.

💰

Support Local Businesses

Choose local restaurants, guides, shops, artists, and accommodations whenever possible.

🎒

Pack Smart

Bring drinking water, sunscreen, comfortable shoes, extra clothes, and basic first-aid supplies.

Be a Responsible Tourist

Travel is not only about seeing beautiful places. It is also about protecting them for future generations.
